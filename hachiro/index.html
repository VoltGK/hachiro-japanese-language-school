Who we are

A community built around the Japanese language

Hachiro Japanese Language School is an educational institute privately owned and operated in Battaramulla, Koswatta. Originally established through tuition sessions three years ago, Hachiro has grown into a training center offering preparation courses for O/L, A/L, and JLPT/NAT (N5/N4) examinations — alongside speaking courses and guidance for higher studies in Japan.

Message from the principal

Learning a language is learning to see the world differently

Every student who walks through our doors carries a different reason for choosing Japanese — an exam, a career, a dream of studying abroad. Our role is to meet them there, and guide them the rest of the way with patience, structure, and genuine care.
